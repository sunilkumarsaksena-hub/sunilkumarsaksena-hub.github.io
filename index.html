Professional Introduction

Design with purpose.

I am a creative and detail-oriented Senior Graphic Designer with more than 20 years of experience in advertising, branding, print production and institutional communication. My work covers the complete design process—from developing concepts and visual direction to preparing final, production-ready artwork. I create brochures, admission campaigns, hoardings, social media creatives, marketing collateral and presentation materials for academic and corporate audiences. Alongside a strong eye for typography, layout and visual consistency, I bring hands-on pre-press knowledge, print-vendor coordination and a practical understanding of delivering high-quality work to deadlines.
